About us

Who's us?

Your friendly neighborhood Coffee Shop.

Brewed with Love.

Hele began in 2020 as a small coffee gathering

In 2022 we opened the doors of our first small shop, with the coffee that is served to neighbors and friends.

Today we still keep things small, warm, friendly, but intimate. Because we treat our customers as Family.

Mission & values

To serve honest, comforting, and carefully made coffee and pastries in a place where everyone feels welcome to stay.

  • 01

    Quality first

    We choose good beans, fresh ingredients and brew every drink to order.

  • 02

    Made fresh

    Pastries are baked in small batches each morning, never stocked for days.

  • 03

    Community

    Students, neighbors and travelers are all welcome. Take the table you need.
